# CareEcho 90-second demo script

## 0–10 seconds — Positioning

**Voice-over:** “Healthcare does not stop when an appointment ends, but patients are expected to remember every symptom and every instruction. CareEcho is a patient-owned voice agent that becomes their health memory.”

**On screen:** Landing hero, then enter the deterministic demo as Stellamaris.

## 10–25 seconds — Longitudinal memory

**Voice-over:** “Stellamaris has reported dizziness four times. CareEcho keeps each dated report and the patient's original words—not a diagnosis.”

**On screen:** Home dashboard showing four dizziness reports, then the four dated timeline entries.

## 25–45 seconds — Consultation context

**Voice-over:** “During a consultation, and only with consent, CareEcho uses AssemblyAI to capture the conversation, speaker context, and timestamps that become the evidence boundary.”

**On screen:** Visit consent, Visit Mode, then **Load demo recording**. Keep the sample label visible.

**Say/type in Ask Ada:** “When did I first report dizziness?”

**Ada:** “Your first saved dizziness report was September 18. You've reported it four times since.”

## 45–65 seconds — Safety and conflict detection

**Doctor statement:** “Change the metformin to 500 milligrams twice daily.”

**Voice-over:** “CareEcho does not silently overwrite health memory. It finds the previous confirmed instruction—500 milligrams once daily—and marks the new instruction as proposed.”

**On screen:** Conflict card with Previous and Proposed values, plus Confirm, Correct, and Don't save.

## 65–80 seconds — Confirm and evidence

**Voice-over:** “Only the patient's confirmation commits the change. The prior instruction is preserved as superseded.”

**Action:** Tap **Confirm**, then **View source**.

**On screen:** `CONFIRMED`, the strong Instruction Changed card, and the exact doctor quote with timestamp.

## 80–90 seconds — Close

**Say/type:** “Ada, what changed with my medication today?”

**Ada:** “Today's confirmed instruction changed from metformin 500 milligrams once daily to 500 milligrams twice daily.”

**Voice-over:** “CareEcho: no source, no claim; no confirmation, no clinical memory change. Your health memory, in your voice.”
