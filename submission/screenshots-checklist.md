# Real screenshot checklist

Do not use mockups or generated screenshots. Capture the deployed app after the final commit is live.

- [ ] **Landing hero** — CareEcho positioning, Ada, and primary CTA visible.
- [ ] **Home with Ada** — Stellamaris, Ada, Talk to Ada, and Dizziness · 4 reports.
- [ ] **Symptom timeline** — Sep 18, 21, 25, and 29 dizziness entries with patient provenance.
- [ ] **Live voice interaction** — Ada in the listening state with calm status text and waveform.
- [ ] **Visit mode** — consent-confirmed recording screen with minimal controls.
- [ ] **Instruction conflict detection** — previous metformin 500 mg once daily vs proposed 500 mg twice daily.
- [ ] **Confirm-to-Commit** — proposed card with Confirm, Correct, and Don't save actions.
- [ ] **Post-visit memory** — Instruction Changed card in `CONFIRMED` state.
- [ ] **Evidence source** — source sheet showing Doctor, timestamp, exact quote, and what CareEcho saved.
- [ ] **Ask Ada answer** — “What changed with my medication today?” with a linked clinician citation.

For every capture: hide browser bookmarks/personal tabs, confirm the sample/demo badge is visible where applicable, check spelling, use consistent viewport dimensions, and avoid cropping controls.
